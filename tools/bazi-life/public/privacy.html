隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 29 日

簡單說：生日、名字和家人的資料都在你的手機（瀏覽器）裡計算，網站不保存。我們只統計匿名的次數。沒有帳號、沒有 cookie、沒有廣告，也不賣或分享任何資料。

誰經營這個網站

「命盤人生」（https://bazi-life.mingpan.workers.dev）是一位在臺灣的個人開發者製作、經營的免費網站，英文網站 Written in the Stars 也是同一位作者。有任何問題請寫信到：（聯絡信箱即將公布）。

在你的裝置上計算的資料

你輸入的出生日期、時間、出生地、性別、姓名，家人（爸爸、媽媽、兄弟姊妹）和伴侶的名字與生日，合婚分頁的兩個人，以及準爸媽模式的姓氏和候選名字，都只在你的瀏覽器裡使用。排盤、人生模擬、同盤重跑 100 次、八字命理詳批、姓名學、家人合盤、合婚和準爸媽比較，全部在你的手機或電腦上計算，不會送到我們的伺服器。

會送出的資料

  • 匿名的次數。網頁會送出固定清單裡的事件名稱（例如「打開網頁」「開始人生」「走完一生」「按了某個按鈕」）、畫面停留的秒數，以及玩的時候每分鐘一次的「心跳」（首頁「此刻在過人生」的人數）。伺服器只把它們加成每天的總數。不含生日、姓名或任何編號。
  • 走完一生的匿名摘要。走完一生時另外送一筆：出生年代（例如「80 後」）、日柱天干、這一局活到幾歲、結婚幾次、有沒有買房、幾個孩子。只保留最近 500 筆，用在首頁的「剛剛走完的人生」和今天的統計。不含生日、姓名、出生地、性別，也沒有編號，無法對回任何人。
  • 「寫下你自己的決定」那一句話。你在抉擇下方自己打字時，那一句話會連同這個抉擇的題目、選項和情境（年齡、工作、感情狀態）送到我們的伺服器，再交給 Cloudflare Workers AI 判讀成遊戲裡的一個行動。送出前，你輸入的名字（自己、家人、伴侶）都會換成「你」「爸爸」「伴侶」這樣的稱呼；不會送出生日。伺服器不保存這句話和判讀結果。提到傷害自己或別人的句子在手機上就會攔下，不會送出。
  • AI 命理分析報告與 AI 深度合婚（尚未開放）。這兩個功能還在準備中：現在按下按鈕只會顯示「敬請期待」，並記一次匿名點擊，不會送出任何生日資料。開放之後，按下時會把你的出生日期、時間、出生地和性別（合婚另含對方的出生日期、時間、性別和出生地）送到我們的伺服器，交給 Cloudflare Workers AI 寫解讀；不送姓名、暱稱、家人或這一局遊戲的內容。伺服器不保存寫好的解讀，也不保存生日；為了少算幾次，最近算過的命盤只會在伺服器的記憶體裡短暫留著，伺服器重新啟動就清掉。開放前，這一段會先更新，網頁上也會寫清楚送出什麼。

主機與記錄

網站架在 Cloudflare（Cloudflare, Inc.）的 Workers 上，匿名的每日總數和走完一生的摘要存在 Cloudflare 的 Durable Objects。和所有網站一樣，Cloudflare 需要處理你的 IP 位址才能把網頁送到你的裝置並防止濫用。我們的程式不記錄 IP，只在當下用它限制每分鐘的次數。Cloudflare 會保留請求記錄（例如時間、網址、狀態碼和連線的技術資料）最多 7 天，用於除錯與防止濫用。詳見 Cloudflare 的隱私權政策。

字型（Google Fonts）

網頁的字型（Noto Sans TC、霞鶩文楷 LXGW WenKai TC、IBM Plex Mono）從 Google Fonts 載入：你的瀏覽器會直接向 Google 的伺服器（fonts.googleapis.com、fonts.gstatic.com）下載字型，所以 Google 會看到你的 IP 位址和瀏覽器資訊。詳見 Google 的隱私權政策。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: your birth data, names and family details are computed on your phone (in the browser) and never stored by the site. We count anonymous events only. No accounts, no cookies, no ads, and we never sell or share data.

Who runs this site

BaZi Life (命盤人生, https://bazi-life.mingpan.workers.dev) is a free website made and run by an independent developer in Taiwan, who also runs the English-language site Written in the Stars. Questions: (contact address coming soon).

What is computed on your device

The birth date, time, place and sex you enter, your name, the names and birthdays of your family (parents, brothers and sisters) and partner, both people on the Match tab, and the surname and candidate names in Baby mode are used only in your browser. The chart, the life simulation, the 100 reruns, the BaZi reading, the name reading, the family and partner charts, Match and the Baby comparison are all computed on your phone or computer and are not sent to our server.

What is sent

  • Anonymous counts. The page sends the names of events from a fixed list (for example "page opened", "life started", "life finished", "a button was pressed"), the number of seconds the page was on screen, and while you play, a "heartbeat" once a minute (for the "playing now" number on the welcome page). The server only adds them to daily totals. No birth date, name or identifier.
  • An anonymous summary of a finished life. When a life ends, one more record is sent: the decade of birth (for example "born in the '80s"), the day stem, the age reached in this game, the number of marriages, whether the character bought a home, and the number of children. Only the latest 500 are kept, for "lives just lived" and today's numbers on the welcome page. It has no birth date, name, place, sex or identifier, so it cannot be traced to anyone.
  • The "write your own decision" sentence. When you type your own decision under a choice, that sentence goes to our server with the choice's question, its options and the situation (age, work, relationship), and is read by Cloudflare Workers AI into one action in the game. Before it is sent, the names you entered (yours, your family's, your partner's) are replaced with words like "you", "Dad" or "partner"; no birth date is sent. The server does not keep the sentence or the result. Sentences about hurting yourself or others are caught on your phone and never sent.
  • The AI chart report and the AI match reading (not open yet). These are still being built: today the button only shows "Coming soon" and counts one anonymous press; no birth data is sent. Once they open, pressing the button will send your birth date, time, place and sex (for a match, also the other person's birth date, time, sex and place) to our server, to be written up by Cloudflare Workers AI; no names, nicknames, family or game content. The server stores neither the reading nor the birth data; to save work, the last charts it computed stay briefly in the server's memory and are gone when it restarts. Before they open, this section will be updated and the page will say exactly what is sent.

Hosting and logs

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.); the anonymous daily totals and the finished-life summaries are stored in Cloudflare Durable Objects. Like any website, Cloudflare has to process your IP address to deliver the page and to stop abuse. Our code does not record IP addresses; it uses them only in the moment, to limit requests per minute. Cloudflare keeps request logs (such as the time, address, status code and technical connection data) for up to 7 days, for debugging and abuse prevention. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

The page's typefaces (Noto Sans TC, LXGW WenKai TC, IBM Plex Mono) load from Google Fonts: your browser downloads them directly from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), so Google sees your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
