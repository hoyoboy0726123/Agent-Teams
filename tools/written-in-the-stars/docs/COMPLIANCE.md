# US compliance notes (research, Oct 2026)

Research, not legal advice; consult a US lawyer before charging. Points marked ⚠ are unverified or jurisdiction-dependent.

## Before a free launch
1. Privacy policy and terms of service (contents below).
2. "For entertainment only" on the landing page, the results page and the footer:
   > A game for entertainment and self-reflection only. Astrology has no proven scientific validity. Nothing here
   > predicts your future or is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
3. AI decision box: label the output as AI-generated, name the AI provider in the privacy policy, and on self-harm
   phrases show 988 (call or text 988, chat at 988lifeline.org) without simulating it.
4. Analytics without cookies, localStorage IDs, fingerprinting or IP logging (then no EU consent banner and no COPPA trigger).
5. Swiss Ephemeris only in test fixtures, never shipped; include the astronomy-engine MIT notice.
6. **Name clearance: "100 Lives" is already a life-sim roguelike (Gamebook Entertainment / Tycoon Publishing, PC/Mac,
   https://nodal.gg/game/100-lives-3864430).** Renamed to **Written in the Stars**; still search USPTO classes 9 and 41 before charging.
7. Basic WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard, contrast, labels, alt text. No accessibility overlays.

## Before charging
1. Use a merchant of record (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy): it handles US sales tax and EU/UK VAT. Confirm in writing that an
   automated AI-written chart report is allowed (both ban human "services"). Stripe lists psychic/fortune-telling as
   restricted ⚠ and may not onboard Taiwan sellers ⚠.
2. Show the disclaimer at checkout (before payment) and on every paid report or PDF.
3. Subscriptions must meet ROSCA and California's ARL (AB 2863, July 2025):
   - price and renewal terms next to the button;
   - a separate opt-in for auto-renewal;
   - a confirmation email;
   - one-click online cancellation;
   - annual reminders;
   - no trial-price tricks.

   The FTC click-to-cancel rule was vacated; new rulemaking began in March 2026.
   Lesson from FTC v. Genesis Tech (Nebula/OBRIO, N.D. Cal. 26-cv-05232, 2026): a "$5 reading" that hid a $45-per-30-days
   subscription, with cancellation made hard.
4. Taiwan tax and registration for cross-border digital sales: ask a Taiwan accountant ⚠.
5. Terms: refunds, EU 14-day withdrawal (consent to immediate delivery), AI limitations, buyers 18+.

## Privacy policy contents
- What is collected: anonymous counts, and the AI text, which is sent but not stored.
- What stays on the device: the birth data.
- Processors: Cloudflare, the AI provider, the merchant of record.
- No sale or sharing of data.
- A section on children.
- User rights and a contact address.
- The policy's date.

CalOPPA applies to any commercial site collecting PI from Californians. CCPA thresholds ($26.6M revenue, 100k consumers) are far off.

## Other
- **Fortune-telling laws:**
  - NY Penal Law §165.35 exempts fortune telling done "solely for entertainment"; repeal bills are pending in 2026.
  - Pennsylvania §7104 is under challenge.
  - These laws are enforced against in-person scams, so the risk is low for a game run from abroad.
- **FTC Act:** make no accuracy, prediction or "scientific" claims. The fake-reviews rule (16 CFR 465) applies.
- **AI laws:**
  - California SB 243 and NY GBL Art. 47 cover companion chatbots. A one-shot interpreter box is probably not one ⚠, but a future chat feature may be.
  - Utah requires disclosing AI use when asked.
- **App stores:** position the app as a game (Apple 4.3 calls astrology "saturated"). Expect a 13+ rating.
- **Health content:** health outcomes are fiction, not predictions. Follow safe messaging on suicide: no methods.
- **Public data:** US government statistics are public domain (17 U.S.C. §105). Cite them.
